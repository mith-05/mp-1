Projects

Proximity Ligation Assay Optimization

Boston University Pratt Laboratory

Investigating methods to improve proximity ligation assays through optimization of antibody-DNA conjugation and linker chemistry. I use digital droplet PCR (ddPCR) to quantify ligation products and evaluate assay performance.

Pancreatic Cancer ddPCR Assay

Worked on the development and optimization of a multiplex ddPCR assay for detecting cancer-associated KRAS and GNAS mutations. My work included preparing samples, performing ddPCR experiments, and analyzing droplet fluorescence data.

Ligation Reaction Optimization

Studied how experimental variables including ligase concentration, reaction time, temperature, and probe-to-template ratio affect DNA ligation efficiency. ddPCR was used to quantify the results of each experimental condition.

JavaScript Calculator

An interactive calculator developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The calculator performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and exponentiation.
